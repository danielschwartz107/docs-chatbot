# Outages and Troubleshooting

## How do I check if there is an outage in my area?
Visit status.brightline.example or open the Outages tab in the account portal and enter your ZIP code. Known outages show an estimated restore time that is updated every 30 minutes.

## My internet is down but there is no outage listed. What should I do?
First restart your modem and router: unplug both for 60 seconds, plug the modem back in, wait until its "Online" light is solid, then plug the router back in. If that does not help, check that every cable is firmly connected and run the line test in the account portal under Support > "Test my connection".

## Will I get a credit for an outage?
Yes. If an outage lasts more than 24 consecutive hours, you get a credit for each full day of lost service. Credits are applied automatically to your next bill for outages listed on the status page. For outages that were not listed, open a support ticket.

## Why is my Wi-Fi slow?
Slow Wi-Fi is usually caused by distance from the router, walls, or interference. Place the router in a central, open spot, use the 5 GHz network for devices close to the router, and restart the router weekly. Plans above 500 Mbps need a Wi-Fi 6 router to reach full speed.

## How do I book a technician?
If the line test reports a signal problem, you can schedule a technician visit in the portal under Support > "Book a technician". Visits are free when the problem is on our network and $75 when it is caused by customer equipment or inside wiring.
