# Billing

## When is my bill due?
Bills are issued on the 1st of each month and are due on the 15th. If the 15th falls on a weekend or holiday, the due date moves to the next business day.

## What payment methods do you accept?
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, debit cards, and ACH bank transfers. You can also pay by check by mail, but checks take 5 to 7 business days to post.

## Is there a late fee?
Yes. Payments received after the due date are charged a $7 late fee. If an account is more than 30 days past due, service may be suspended until the balance is paid.

## How do I set up autopay?
Sign in to the Brightline account portal, open Billing, choose "Autopay", and add a card or bank account. Customers on autopay with paperless billing get a $5 monthly discount.

## Why is my first bill higher than expected?
Your first bill usually includes a partial month of service (prorated from your installation date), the full upcoming month, and a one-time $49 installation fee if a technician visited.

## How do I dispute a charge?
Contact support within 60 days of the bill date. Open a ticket in the account portal under Billing > "Dispute a charge" and include the bill date and the line item. Disputed amounts are not subject to late fees while under review.
