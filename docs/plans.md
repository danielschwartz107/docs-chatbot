# Plans and Account

## What internet plans do you offer?
- Basic 200: 200 Mbps download, $45 per month.
- Plus 500: 500 Mbps download, $60 per month.
- Gig 1000: 1 Gbps download, $80 per month.
All plans include unlimited data and no annual contract.

## Can I change my plan?
Yes. You can upgrade at any time in the account portal under Account > "Change plan" and the new speed starts within 2 hours. Downgrades take effect at the start of your next billing cycle.

## How do I move my service to a new address?
Open a ticket under Account > "Move service" at least 14 days before your move. We check availability at the new address and schedule installation there. Moving is free once per year.

## How do I cancel my service?
Call support or open a cancellation ticket in the portal. There is no cancellation fee. Rented equipment must be returned within 30 days or a $120 unreturned-equipment fee applies.

## Do you rent equipment?
Yes. Modem rental is $10 per month and the Wi-Fi 6 router is $8 per month. You can also use your own compatible modem; see the approved list in the portal.
